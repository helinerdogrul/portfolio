About

I’m Helin Erdoğrul, a designer and art director interested in creating visual worlds where ideas, culture, and emotion come together.

My practice moves across art direction, brand identity, editorial design, digital experiences, and moving image. I approach each project as a narrative system, exploring how image, typography, motion, and context can shape the way a story is experienced.

Alongside commercial collaborations, I develop self-initiated projects that allow me to experiment with new formats and ways of seeing. I’m especially drawn to work that begins with curiosity and grows through research, observation, and visual experimentation.

My process is guided by strong concepts, thoughtful details, and a desire to create work that feels both clear and unexpected.
